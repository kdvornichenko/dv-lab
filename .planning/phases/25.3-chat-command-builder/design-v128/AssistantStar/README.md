# AssistantStar

The assistant as a small yellow star that floats over the app, in place of the chat button of the top bar: a click opens a one-line field beside it, and after the first message the chat appears as a block over the field. The owner's answers on `ChatPanel`, the phone and the narrow band are in "Relationship to `ChatPanel`".

**You provide:** nothing per screen; the shell owns it. It keeps its position, its open conversation, its draft and a reply that is being written while the teacher moves between pages.

## The character
- **Source of the motion.** `FrostCloud.tsx` of the owner (`/private/tmp/claude-501/-Volumes-T7-personal-dv-lab/120897d7-c68e-4327-b8b1-3f1dfe5726c9/scratchpad/frost-cloud/FrostCloud.tsx`; the file is outside the repository, copy it in when building): the loop, the springs and the idle behaviour are taken as they are. It floats (a sine of 5px at 1.1 rad/s), breathes (scale 1.2%), glances at one of six directions every 1.4 to 3.6s, blinks every 2.4 to 5.4s (one blink in four is double), follows the pointer with its eyes (spring 70 / damping 13, the gaze clamped to the eyes' range), squashes and winks on a press (spring 220 / damping 9, the wink 120ms after it), runs only while it is on screen and the tab is visible (IntersectionObserver and `visibilitychange`), and keeps still under `prefers-reduced-motion`. **What changes is the shape and the palette:** no frost, sky, halo or goo filter; the star is one path.
- **Shape.** A chubby five-pointed star in a 240 box: points on a circle of 92, the dips on 51, the corners rounded by a stroke of the body colour, a 3px outline in `--star-edge`, a soft white glint on the upper left arm. Two tall black oval eyes (9 by 15.5, 36 apart) a little below the middle with a white glint each, the look of Mario's star; no mouth, no cheeks. It is `background=false`: there is no sky, no card, no plate behind it, only a soft shadow under it (`--star-shadow`) that shrinks and fades as it floats up.
- **Size.** 56px on the screen (the 240 box scaled), a hit area of 56px, never under 44px on touch.
- **Colour** (v126, owner: yellow). Variant A has no brand hue and keeps headlines, rows and buttons neutral, so this is a deliberate single exception: the star is the only yellow thing in the app. It is **not `warning`** (amber, reserved for a status). Token pair, light and dark, to be added to the shell's tokens: `--star-top` #ffe45c / #ffe766, `--star-bottom` #ffbf1f / #ffb300, `--star-edge` #c98a00 / #8a5a00, `--star-eye` #1a1406, `--star-glint` #ffffff, `--star-shadow` rgba(0,0,0,.28) / rgba(0,0,0,.55). The body is a vertical gradient from top to bottom. The star is decoration with a name: the button is "Assistant" and the drawing is `aria-hidden`.

## States
| State | What the star does |
|---|---|
| **Rest** (default) | In the bottom right corner, 16px from the edges (`--unit` from 768px, 8px under it) above the safe area; floats, breathes, glances, blinks, follows the pointer across the window. |
| **Pressed** | A click: squashes (scale 1.1 by 0.86 from the bottom) and winks, then the field opens. |
| **Dragged** | A pointer or finger moving more than 4px (8px on touch) starts a drag; the star leans toward the move (rotation by the velocity, at most 9 degrees, on a spring), lifts (the shadow grows and fades), looks where it is going, and follows the pointer 1:1. It never snaps to an edge or a corner; it is kept 8px inside the window (safe area included) and re-fitted when the window changes. Where it is let go, it stays. |
| **Field open** | Stays where it is, turns its eyes to the field, goes on floating; a click on it closes the field. |
| **The model is working** | Looks up and to the left and rocks side to side (rotation 7 degrees, 1.2s) until the text starts, then goes back to resting; the field shows Stop reply and `ChatProgress` carries the words. |
| **Reply ready** | One hop (up 14px and back on the `moderate` spring) and a wink, once. |
| **Closed, a reply arrived** (v124) | The `info` badge and, once, the bubble (below); the hop and wink come with the bubble. |
| **Closed, a decision waits** (v124) | The `warning` badge; it stays until the decision is made. A hop only when the proposal appears. |

- **Badge.** A 12px circle at the star's upper right with a 2px ring in the page colour, outside the silhouette so it reads on the yellow; `info` and `warning` as on the chat button of `ChatPanel`, `warning` wins and carries a dark "!" so that, amber beside yellow (in the dark theme `warning` is nearly the star's yellow), it is not read as a speck of the star; the Chat row of the sidebar carries the same dot as before. The badge goes when the field opens (the `info` one) or when the decision is made (the `warning` one).
- **Bubble** (the toast of v124 for this flow, because the corner is the star's). A card of the toast's look (`surface-4`, `shadow-4`, 12px radius, 288px, `role="status"`, polite), beside the star on the side with room, with a 10px tail toward it: the 16px icon in `info` or `warning`, "Reply is ready" or "Waiting for your decision", the conversation's title in `caption muted`, **Open chat** (compact secondary, it opens the field with that conversation). It goes after 4s; the badge stays. Never opens while the field or the Chat page shows the reply. The push for a tab that is not in front is unchanged (`SettingsNotifications`, off by default).
- **Reduced motion.** No float, breath, glance, blink or rocking; the star stands still, the eyes straight. A press and the hop are replaced by nothing; a drag still works and is the pointer 1:1. The rest of the states are carried by words and the badge.

## Moving it
- **Pointer and touch.** The star is a `button` (`touch-action: none` so a finger drags it instead of scrolling); a move under the threshold is a click.
- **Where it is kept.** On this device (`localStorage`), as fractions of the window, so it survives a reload and a resize; the first time it is the corner. A clamp on every resize.
- **Keyboard.** It is in the tab order; Enter or Space opens the field, the arrow keys move it by 16px (64px with Shift) while it has focus. Nothing more is promised for keyboard use.
- **Above and below.** Over the page, the sidebar and the top bar; **under** every dialog, its backdrop, the search palette and the update notice's place (the notice sits at the opposite side by itself); hidden on the Chat page, the exam room, the sign-in page and the student's shell (the assistant is the teacher's).

## The field
- A one-row pill, `surface-3`, `shadow-3`, 48px high, full radius, 560px (the window less 16px at most), beside the star on the side with more room, its bottom on the star's bottom line. The placeholder is the app's own, "Type / for commands or @ for a student" (`subtitle`, `muted-foreground`); Enter sends, Shift+Enter makes a line (up to five, the field grows away from the screen edge), `/` and `@` open the commands and the mention list above the field (`surface-4`) exactly as in `ChatComposer`.
- At the end: the **chat chip** (outline compact, the conversation's title, 150px at most, a chevron) and the round **send** button, 32px, `primary`, the up arrow (off at 50% while the field is empty); while the model works it is **Stop reply**, a secondary round button with the stop icon and an `aria-label`. The chip's menu opens upward (`surface-4`, `shadow-4`, 288px, 40px rows): **New chat** (plus), the four latest conversations (title, time), a hairline, **All chats** (goes to the Chat page). The model and the effort are those of the last chat; they are changed on the Chat page.
- It opens with focus in the field. Esc closes the innermost layer (a popover, the chip's menu), then the block and the field together; the draft and the conversation stay. A click elsewhere does not close it: it is not a dialog.

## The block
- **Appears with the first message.** Over the field when the star is in the lower half of the window (below it when in the upper half), 8px from it, the same width, `surface-3`, `shadow-3`, `radius-2xl`, entering with opacity and an 8px rise on `moderate`. Header 40px: the conversation's title (`subtitle` 600) and three icon buttons, **Open as panel** (the conversation moves into `ChatPanel`; not drawn under 1024px), **Open in chat** (the Chat page on this conversation) and **Close**. Under it the conversation of `ChatPage` in a 528px column: the messages anchored to the field, a 48px fade at the top, every block of the chat (`ChatFacts`, `ChatCalendarChange`, `ChangeCard`, the notices) unchanged. Its height follows the messages up to 60% of the window height and 560px, then it scrolls.
- **Phone.** The field is the screen's width less 8px each side, at the bottom above the keyboard (the visual viewport) and the safe area; the block over it up to the top bar; the star sits on the block's upper right corner while they are open, then returns.

## Relationship to `ChatPanel` (answered by the owner, v127)
Answered 2026-10-16 through Main bro: **the star replaces only the chat button of the top bar; `ChatPanel` stays.** The star is on every screen that has the shell, **the phone and the 1024 to 1191px band included**.
- **The button is gone, the panel is not.** The top bar has no chat button; the panel (`ChatPanel`, its widths, motion and content) is opened from the star's block with **Open as panel** (the first of the block's three header buttons, the sidebar icon mirrored): the same conversation moves into the panel, the field and the block close, and the star hides while the panel is open (the panel is the chat); it comes back on the spot where it was when the panel closes. Under 1024px there is no panel, so the button is not drawn there; **Open in chat** (the Chat page) is the way out.
- **Signals.** What `ChatPanel` gave to its button moves onto the star: the badge (`info`, `warning`), the bubble in this flow, the hop and the wink. The dot on the Chat row of the sidebar stays; the push and the Settings switch are unchanged.
- **Answered (owner, 2026-10-16, v128).** **`]` is only the panel's key:** it opens and closes `ChatPanel` as `[` does the sidebar (on the last conversation when it opens from nothing; ignored in a field; nothing under 1024px), and never touches the star's field. The star opens by click, or with Enter or Space when it has the focus. **The bubble replaces the toast** wherever the star exists: no "Reply is ready" toast is shown, the star hops, winks and shows the bubble and the badge; the toast of `ChatPanel` is not built (on `/chat`, with the panel open and in the exam room there is nothing to announce or no chat).
- **Still the owner's:** whether the panel needs another way to open when the star is far from where the teacher looks (the Chat row of the sidebar opens the Chat page, not the panel).

## Roles
The star is a `button` with `aria-label="Assistant"`, `aria-expanded`, `aria-controls` the field; the field is a `role="dialog"` that is not modal, labelled "Assistant", no focus trap; the block's messages are `role="log"`.
