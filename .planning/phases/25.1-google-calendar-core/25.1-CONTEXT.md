# Phase 25.1: Google Calendar Core — Context

**Gathered:** 2026-10-12
**Status:** Ready for research and planning. Replaces Phase 25 (archived in `.planning/archive/phase-25-superseded`, kept only as a source of provider facts).

<domain>

## Phase Boundary

The schedule becomes a two-way Google Calendar for any event, not only lessons. The teacher creates, moves, resizes, edits and deletes events in the app and sees changes made in Google without an open page. Google wins when both sides changed. Lessons stay the app's own model; every other event is a plain calendar event.

Outbound commands, the read model, the connection and the account switch already exist from Phase 24 and the calendar PRs 10-29. This phase rewrites the background synchronization and adds the editing core. Chat (22, 23) and IELTS (26) stay outside.

</domain>

<decisions>

## Locked Decisions (owner, 2026-10-12)

- **D-01 Order:** the sync rewrite comes first, then the editing core, then the calendar choice; chat follows after this phase.
- **D-02 Sync rewrite:** one background task per connection with no shared account-wide lock, the week version grows only on real changes, a bounded sync window, cleanup of stale rows, no tables beyond what the model needs. Push notifications trigger incremental sync with `syncToken`; a real `410` triggers a full resync with no duplicate lessons; channels are renewed before expiry; a nightly reconcile recovers missed delivery.
- **D-03 Editing core:** create any event; move and resize with the mouse; delete one occurrence, a whole series, or this and following; repeats; colors; description and place. Views: week only. Day and month views are not part of this phase.
- **D-04 Calendar choice:** the account can pick a calendar inside the connected Google account (PRIMARY stays the default).
- **D-05 Stored fields:** keep only the Google fields the app needs; no raw event copies.
- **D-06 Other events:** events that are not lessons are shown as events with title, time and Google color; they can be created, moved, resized and deleted like any other event.
- **D-07 Name match:** an event whose title matches a student name is only suggested; the teacher binds it with Assign or discards it with Ignore. Nothing is bound automatically. App-created events map back to their lessons by stored id first.
- **D-08 Google wins:** when the same item changed in both places the Google version remains after sync. Marks and accounting history of past lessons are never rewritten by inbound edits.
- **D-09 Event length:** up to 1440 minutes (one day); longer events exist only as all-day events (`eventMinutes` in `packages/contracts/src/calendar.ts`, owner decision 2026-10-13). The 15-240 CHECK was removed in PR 32. The schedule day runs 02:00-02:00 (PR 25).
- **D-10 No hard delete in schedule tables:** rows change status, `kind` or `ends_on`. Series rule columns stay immutable after insert; a cut gives a new series (see AGENTS.md).
- **D-11 Testing:** live Google checks only locally on the owner test account with a local OAuth client; prod holds the teacher's working calendar and receives releases without live testing. No new unit or e2e tests; acceptance by scripts, logs and the browser.
- **D-12 Runtime:** Claude only, no Codex. Server work goes through Server guy, design through Design dude.
- **D-13 Calendar binding:** lessons and events are bound to the calendar they were created or matched in. When the account picks another calendar, items bound to the previous one stay untouched on the app side and are hidden from the grid.
- **D-14 Scope of edits for lessons:** two scopes, this occurrence and this and following (the existing cut and End series); no "all" for lessons, past history is never rewritten (amended by D-21: move and resize also have "all", realised as a cut from the first changeable occurrence). Plain events get this, this and following, all.
- **D-15 All-day events:** shown as a narrow strip above the grid; the only action is delete; no move or resize.
- **D-16 Repeats:** only weekly repeats are supported; other Google repeat rules are shown read-only (a single occurrence may still be moved or deleted).
- **D-17 Assign and Ignore:** apply to the whole recurring series. Ignore hides the suggestion and keeps the event as a plain event; it is not undoable in this phase, a manual Assign stays available in the event details at any time.
- **D-18 Assign mark:** Assign writes a private extended property with the lesson id into the Google event, so the link survives reconnection and a full resync (`410`). The UI-SPEC statement that Assign writes nothing to Google is superseded by this decision.
- **D-19 Sync window:** 8 weeks back and 53 weeks forward; weeks outside the window are read-only. Rows of bound lessons are never cleaned, so lessons up to 366 days back can still be marked. Reads use only SQL over the projection, never Google and never the account lease; the lease stays for command sending and connect/disconnect. DELETE is granted only on the projection cache table; it stays revoked on the other new tables.
- **D-20 Push verification:** a temporary tunnel to the local stack with the owner test account and a local OAuth client (the production callback is hard-coded in `apps/api/src/config.ts` and must become configurable for local runs). Prod and the working calendar are not touched.
- **D-21 Mechanics review, 2026-10-12 (owner answers):**
  - A Google repeat added by hand for a student who already has an app series is a plain event with a suggested Assign; it never merges with the app series. One student can hold any number of series; Assign binds one Google series to one lesson series.
  - Move and resize of a lesson or series ask for the scope each time, like Google: this occurrence, this and following, all. This amends D-14 (lessons had no "all").
  - Scope "all" for lessons moves every occurrence that is future or has no mark; past occurrences with a lesson mark stay at their old time and keep their marks and balance (history is not rewritten, D-08). Series rule columns stay immutable (D-10): "all" is realised as a cut, the planner decides the mechanics and where the marks stay.
  - A mark exists only on a lesson that has already started (the mark action is refused before the start), so a future lesson never carries a mark and Google wins on it without exception. A lesson that has started is frozen against inbound moves and cancels (D-08). The mark stays on our lesson or occurrence (series id and original date), not on the Google event, so it survives recreation of the event, `410` and calendar switch.
  - A split of a series made in Google (this and following) is verified live: whether the private marker is copied into the new master decides if the second half needs a new Assign.
  - A series keeps the zone it was created in (teacher_settings default applies only to new series, phase 21.1); recurrence and inbound moves are interpreted in the series zone.
  - Group lessons (several names in one title) are not handled in this phase; such an event stays a plain event.

<specifics>

## Success Criteria

1. An event created, moved, edited or deleted in Google appears in the schedule shortly after with no app page open; a `410` leads to a full resync with no duplicate lessons; a change made while notifications were not delivered appears after the nightly reconcile.
2. Moving one occurrence changes only that occurrence; moving a master changes the series; delete works for one, series and this-and-following.
3. Any event (lesson or not) can be created, moved by mouse, resized by mouse, edited (color, description, place, repeat) and deleted in the app, and the result is what Google holds.
4. App-created events map back by id; title matches are suggested with Assign or Ignore; all other events are plain events.
5. Google wins on concurrent edits; marks and accounting of past lessons stay as they were.
6. The account can switch the calendar inside the connected Google account.
7. One sync task per connection survives restart and does not block week reads; the week version grows only when something changed.

</specifics>
