Зачем · цель фазы и решения владельца Polish of the built product before IELTS (Phase 26): design leftovers, tails of the checks of Phases 22 and 23, chat improvements, Students table, student profile (Overview, Lessons, Payments, Notes, Vocabulary) and installable PWA with push notifications. Design is closed: v96-v101 (code-sync 76-111). Design dude tracks status by item number; after each plan send him the closed numbers. No hard delete: schedule rows change status or ends_on only. For notes: words of the vocabulary are kept 30 days in Removed, then erased; empty blocks may be deleted at once; a block with text is deleted only after confirmation. Testing: no new unit or e2e tests; acceptance by dev-check scripts, logs and the browser. a held lesson takes its duration from the calendar (current length in the schedule), not frozen at the mark. Held lessons are not moved or changed. asking dialogs have no corner X (footer Discard changes / Cancel then the action), showing dialogs have a corner X and no footer Close … scroll-fade, scroll-fade-x, scroll-divider need a fallback without animation-timeline one follow-up button "Count lessons since the last payment" sends at once and disappears from that answer … starts at the first lesson of the debt series before the last payment one tool edits Details fields … several fields in one Confirm/Decline card (ChangeCardDetails), expectedRevision CAS; Name, Status, account and Opening balance are not editable the server decides by diff: new text that keeps every old line and only adds is written at once with a receipt and Undo; any removed, changed, merged, moved line … gives an "Update <block>?" Confirm/Decline card the agent fixes duplicates and typos itself and reports conflicts in the result with one shared Undo … "Review now" in the profile opens a chat with the review already running. only the prompt text of built-in /recap and /essay moves to the database and is editable in Settings sorting and filters in the URL (nuqs) … actions through LessonDialog (mark, move, cancel, restore, open in calendar); held lessons are view only Installable PWA, no offline. Push: "a lesson starts soon" … and "a student is about to pay" (once when the Pays soon threshold is reached); debt and sync failures are not pushed.
